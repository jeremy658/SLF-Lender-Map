Glossary & Benchmarks Terms, why lenders care, and red-flag thresholds
NOI — Net Operating Income

Revenue (leases + additional rent) minus operating expenses, after vacancy (~3%), management (~6%) and structural reserve (~2%). The engine of value and debt service.

Underwrite to the lower of market vs actual rent
DSCR — Debt Service Coverage Ratio

NOI ÷ annual debt service. How comfortably income covers the mortgage.

Investment/multi ≥1.20–1.25x · business ~1.10x · CMHC 1.10x res / 1.40x commercial
Cap Rate

NOI ÷ property value. Value = NOI ÷ cap rate.

Cross-check against CBRE / market cap for the class
LTV — Loan-to-Value

Loan ÷ value. Primary sizing constraint on income property.

75% conv · up to 95% CMHC · 100%+ owner-occupied
LTC — Loan-to-Cost

Loan ÷ total project cost (land + hard + soft). Construction sizes on LTC, not LTV.

65–80% base · 85–90% with presales / land lift
Debt Yield

NOI ÷ loan amount. Lender's leverage-independent risk gauge.

Stabilized ≥8–10% · new-build 11–12%
Interest Reserve

Slice of the construction loan set aside to cover interest-only payments during the build.

Gross-up the loan: factor = 1 − rate − fee
Contingency

Buffer for cost overruns on a build.

~10% of hard + soft costs
Development Spread

(As-complete value − total cost) ÷ cost. The developer's margin.

Lenders want ~15%+ projected profit
Presale Gate

% of units pre-sold a Tier-1 lender requires before advancing past land/first draw.

60–80% typical; drop to B/private if none, refi later
Gross-Up

Sizing the loan up so it covers financing costs (interest reserve + fees), not just build cost.

Loan ÷ (1 − rate − fee)
VTB — Vendor Take-Back

Seller-provided second mortgage; boosts leverage behind the first.

Confirm first lender permits + standstill
CSBFL

Canada Small Business Financing Loan — up to $1.15M gov-guaranteed via a participating lender.

$1M term + $150K LOC · 2% fee · 85% guarantee
MLI Select

CMHC insured-multifamily program; points for affordability / energy / accessibility unlock higher LTV & longer am.

Up to 95% LTV · 30–55yr am · DSCR 1.10
The 5 C's

Character, Capacity (DSCR), Capital (equity/skin), Collateral (security), Conditions (market). The credit lens.

Frame every executive summary around them
RevPAR

ADR × occupancy. Revenue per available room — the hotel top-line health metric.

Compare to STR / CoStar comp set
Recourse

Full (R) / partial (PR) / non-recourse (NR). Whether the borrower is personally on the hook.

Life cos often NR at low LTV
